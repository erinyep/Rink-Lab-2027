# MoneyPuck 2026-27 unblocked shots, November 10 vintage

`shots_2026.zip` is the original MoneyPuck download copied byte-for-byte. It contains 23,578 shot events and 282 validated games as of the November 10, 2026 refresh. Validation coverage, checksums, source URL, license, and reconciliation notes are in `metadata.json`.

Source: [MoneyPuck published downloads](https://moneypuck.com/data.htm). Non-commercial research use; credit MoneyPuck.com. Blocked attempts are absent by source design; downloaded xG is not a historical as-of model score.
