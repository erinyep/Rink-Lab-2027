# Fantasy Draft Lab — Variable Dictionary and Modeling Contracts

Status: **proposed schema, not currently ingested/model-fitted fields**. Target: 2026–27. Companion: [FANTASY_DRAFT_STRATEGY.md](FANTASY_DRAFT_STRATEGY.md).

## How to read the dictionary

- **Feature (F):** measurable at the forecast cutoff and possibly useful for predicting future production.
- **Target (T):** future realized quantity used for model training/validation; must not appear in same-cutoff features.
- **Prediction (P):** generated by a model, with point estimate, uncertainty and model version.
- **League (L):** league rules and roster constraints that translate hockey forecasts into decisions.
- **Decision (D):** modeled value *conditional on league rules, roster and draft state*.
- **Audit (A):** metadata/quality/provenance, never a substitute for evidence of player skill.

A variable's presence below is a modeling proposal, **not** evidence it is available in the repo or predictive. Prefer reproducible NHL public data and documented alternatives; independently inspect any external xG, injury, role, eligibility or ADP license/coverage.

**Grain contract:** observed skater event table = player × game × strength state (plus explicit missing participation); candidate feature snapshots = player × as_of × target season; projection distribution = player × target game or period × scenario; league valuation = player × league settings × roster/draft state × cutoff. Goalies are separate.

## 1. Identity, dates and population

| Variables / role | Definition, units and use | Critical checks |
|---|---|---|
| player_id (A), season_id (A), game_id (A) | NHL IDs; explicit NHL season encoding, game type and game identity | Unique by appropriate grain; season-aware joins; traded players aggregated once per game. |
| player_name, birth_date, age_at_cutoff (F/A) | Human name/display; age at forecast cutoff, not age at later season end | Names not join keys. Avoid later biographical or role data being used at past cutoff. |
| team_as_of, roster_eligible_as_of (F) | Current team and roster/eligibility at historical cutoff | No end-of-season team attached to pretrade dates. |
| platform_player_id, eligible_C/LW/RW/D/G (L) | Actual fantasy platform's ID and potentially multiple eligible slots, captured at draft | Platform eligibility can differ from NHL roster position; track effective dates. |
| as_of_utc, observed_at_utc, effective_at_utc (A) | When a data record was retrieved/known and when the event occurred | Only include features known at cutoff; historical snapshots cannot be rebuilt with future news. |
| source, source_version, retrieval_hash, game_type (A) | Data provenance | Source coverage, score definition, pagination, timestamp, raw snapshot integrity. |
| team_games_scheduled, rest_of_season_games, games_by_week (F) | As-of schedule, dates, known postponements | Team schedule is not player GP. Account for future schedule revisions as uncertainty. |
| data_history_games, data_history_minutes, missingness_flags (A) | Exposure and availability of inputs | Retain low-history players, explicitly tag/shrink/fallback instead of silently dropping them. |

## 2. Availability and opportunity (predict before events)

| Variables / role | Definition, units and use | Model/interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| historical_gp, historical_team_games (F) | Prior appearances / team games while roster eligible | Baseline appearance frequency; separate ineligibility, scratches and unknown missing data. |
| appearance_rolling_5/10/20 (F) | Lagged observed appearances over roster-eligible past games | Windows are candidate choices to validate, not proven optimal thresholds. |
| roster_status, injury_status_observed, days_since_last_appearance (F) | Timestamped information actually known at cutoff | Unknown injury status != healthy; source confidence and stale-news flags required. |
| predicted_appearance_p_ig (P) | P(player appears in future game g) | Calibrate by history length, role and known statuses; no future lineup labels. |
| predicted_gp_mean, gp_p10/p50/p90 (P) | Sum/simulation of appearance indicators across games | Bernoulli appearance events not necessarily independent; planned schedule cannot exceed game ceiling. |
| ev_toi_seconds/game, pp_toi_seconds/game, sh_toi_seconds/game (F) | Lagged TOI conditional on appearance by mutually exclusive source-defined strength groups | Validate EV+PP+SH=total for same definition; do not add overlapping OT separately. |
| toi_trend, toi_rolling_mean, toi_variability (F) | Past usage levels, trends and variability | Exposure-weight, account for injuries/score effects; not inherently player talent. |
| predicted_ev/pp/sh_toi_conditional (P) | Future TOI by state conditional on playing | Sum to conditional projected TOI; convert seconds to minutes consistently. |
| lineup_role, forward_line, defense_pair (F) | Timestamped as-of observational lineup role | Inform uncertain opportunity; source/coach changes; no retrospective "current" role. |
| pp1_probability, pp2_probability, pp_off_probability (P) | Probabilistic power-play deployment | Mutually exclusive, sum to 1 for clearly defined states; PP1 is not guaranteed PP minutes. |
| sh_role_probability (P) | Probability of PK/SH deployment | PK may increase blocks/SH TOI and suppress offensive opportunity; estimate separately. |
| time_on_ice_expected (P) | Per-game conditional TOI × participation probabilities | Do not conflate high per-60 rate with high expected count. |
| team_pp_opportunities, team_sh_minutes, team_pace (F/P) | Lagged team environment and future opportunity | Prior-team stats of traded players require updated assignment; use team forecast not future observed value. |
| power_play_role_stability, probability_role_change (F/P) | Role persistence and uncertainty estimated from past transitions | Do not invent categorical confidence labels; calibrate if possible. |
| game_rest_days, back_to_back, home_away (F) | Known as-of schedule features | Test incremental forecast improvement; especially goalie starts. |
| lineup_game_overlap, active_slot_conflict (L/D) | Number of games likely blocked by another rostered player in same slots | Season GP differs from fantasy **usable starts**. |

**Opportunity decomposition:** for count category k, initial expected total = sum over future games and strength states of P(appearance) × conditional TOI minutes × modeled events/60. Simulate the joint components if appearance, minutes and rates are dependent.

## 3. Scoring and shot-generation features

| Variables / role | Definition / predictive hypothesis | Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| past_goals, goals_per_game, goals_per_60_EV/PP/SH (F) | Recent finishing outcomes conditional on opportunities | Goals are noisy; low-TOI denominator instability; regularize. |
| shots_on_goal, sog_per_60_by_state (F) | Measured on-net shot generation; projects SOG and goal volume | Verify that blocked/missed attempts are not SOG. |
| individual_shot_attempts, attempts_per_60 (F) | Attempts (e.g., Corsi individual if reliable) | Source definitions vary; not itself counted unless league says so. |
| individual_xg, ixg_per_60, xg_per_shot (F) | Shot-quality-adjusted chance generation, where licensed/documented | xG systems not interchangeable; validate incremental performance over shots. |
| shot_distance, angle, location_mix, rebound/slot attempts (F) | Shot mix and location where available | Avoid overfitting tracking data; source and missingness matter. |
| shooting_pct, rolling_sh_pct, goals_minus_xg (F) | Realized conversion and residual vs modeled xG | Shrink toward player/role/context prior; cannot automatically label residual luck. |
| historical_assists, primary_assists_per_60, secondary_assists_per_60 (F) | Observed playmaking; potentially differing persistence | Secondary-assist recording/process and on-ice conversion effects. |
| on_ice_xg_for, shot_assists, creation_proxy (F) | Supporting offense proxies if reliable | On-ice outcomes include teammate/team deployment; not pure individual skill. |
| points_involvement_pct, on_ice_sh_pct (F) | Fraction of team scoring involving player and on-ice conversion | Team context and on-ice luck can dominate; regularize. |
| teammates_as_of, top_line_teammate_share, team_expected_scoring (F/P) | Likely offensive context | Forecast teammates as-of; do not join later actual line mates. |
| pp_points, pp_goals, pp_assists, pp_events_per_60 (F) | Power-play-specific production | Estimate conditional PP events with PP role/TOI; avoid double counting EV+PP. |
| sh_points, sh_goals, sh_assists (F) | Short-handed production | Sparse outcome; conservatively shrink unless league counts. |
| expected_goals, expected_assists, expected_points, expected_ppp (P) | Forecast G/A/P/PPP separately with identities | P=G+A, PPP<=P in each simulation; if P/G/A all count, count them per **actual** scoring rules, but model dependence. |
| expected_sog, sog_count_distribution (P) | Shot count forecast | Often useful independent of goals when SOG scored, but correlated with goals. |

Use chronological evaluation to test whether lagged ixG, SOG, shot attempts and deployment outperform historic goals/points alone. It is a hypothesis, not an assumed result.

## 4. Peripheral and format-specific skater variables

| Variables / role | Definition and construction | Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| hits, hits_per_60_EV/SH (F/P) | Prior hits and strength-aware event rate; future hits | Home/arena scorer effects; role and team pace; league may not count hits. |
| blocks, blocks_per_60_by_state (F/P) | Prior blocked shots and conditional rate; future blocks | Deployment, defensive environment and SH minutes matter. |
| pim_minutes, penalties_taken, penalty_minutes_per_game (F/P) | Past and forecast PIM | Rare major penalties create tail risk; rules sometimes distinguish PIM vs penalties. |
| takeaway, giveaway, scorer_bias_proxy (F/P) | Only if source/scoring supports them | Recorded definitions and scorer bias can be substantial. |
| faceoff_attempts, faceoff_wins, faceoff_win_pct (F) | Faceoffs taken, wins and ratio | Project attempts (role/opportunity) and wins given attempts; wins <= attempts. |
| forecast_fow, forecast_fol (P) | Count outcomes conditional on predicted attempts and success probability | Avoid separate unconstrained regressions giving FOW+FOL>attempts. |
| plus_minus, on_ice_goals_for/against, team_goal_diff (F/P) | Noisy team-dependent plus/minus projection if scored | Strength-state rules and team context; not a direct player skill proxy. |
| sh_points, game_winning_goals, hat_tricks (P) | Sparse bonuses if counted | Require coherent simulation; heavily regularize rare events. |
| shots_blocked_against, penalty_drawn (F/P) | Other custom stats only if exact platform scoring requires them | Confirm platform definition before ingestion. |

No category receives intrinsic fantasy weight if the league doesn't score it. A metric can still help *predict* a scored event without being awarded direct fantasy value.

## 5. Age, career, persistence and environment

| Variables / role | Definition / proposed use | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| age, age_spline, nhl_experience (F) | Nonlinear maturation/decline hypotheses | Fit using prior seasons, do not hand-award "young upside". |
| multi_year_weighted_rate, ewm_rate (F) | Recency-weighted historical production | Select weights via walk-forward validation, no future info. |
| season_to_season_stability, split_half_reliability (A/F) | Estimated repeatability by variable/context | Evaluate exposure, survivor bias and changing role. |
| sample_exposure, shrinkage_weight, prior_rate (A/P) | Empirical Bayes/regularized blend for low-history players | Keep original observations; priors fit training history only. |
| player_team_change, coach_change, pp_role_change (F) | Only known-at-cutoff moves and observable changes | Predict role shock; not automatically positive or negative. |
| competition_for_ice_time, roster_depth (F) | As-of team depth and replacement threats | Document source; likely role uncertainty, not a direct stat bonus. |
| league_scoring_environment, team_pace (F) | Historical era/team context normalization | Do not use future actual league scoring averages to forecast past season. |
| uncertainty_of_GP, rate, role, age_curve (P) | Component forecast dispersion and scenario correlations | Do not sum separate "risk penalties" for an issue already reflected in mean GP. |

## 6. Goalie-specific contract (separate from skaters)

| Variables / role | Definition, unit and purpose | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| team_game_schedule, rest, b2b (F) | Available start opportunities | Team games are not individual goalie starts. |
| starts_last_5/10/20, start_share (F) | Prior workload conditional on team games | Time-varying tandem and roster changes; validate windows. |
| goalie_status_as_of, competing_goalies, coach_usage (F) | Observed role/availability | Injury status unknown != healthy. |
| start_probability_ig, predicted_starts (P) | Game-level start Bernoulli and sum | Calibrate start share; avoid >team games. |
| shots_against_per_start, shots_against_per_60 (F/P) | Future shots faced from team pace/defense/opponent | Do not infer shot quality solely from volume. |
| saves, goals_against, shots_against (F/P) | Event counts; SA = SV + GA in modeled full-game accounting | Keep event and partial-game definitions explicit. |
| save_pct, expected_save_pct, xga/shot (F/P) | SV/SA; quality-adjusted context where available | Strong regression to mean for short history; source comparability. |
| gaa, toi_goalie_minutes (F/P) | 60 × GA / goalie minutes | GAA is not GA per start; aggregate by minutes. |
| team_win_prob, win_given_start, expected_wins (F/P) | Team support + goalie start outcomes | Goalie wins not identical to team wins; account for scoring context. |
| shutout_rate, shutout_probability (F/P) | Low-base-rate outcome if league counts | Regularization; coherent with goals-allowed simulation. |
| goalie_fantasy_min_starts, max_starts (L) | Qualification and roster constraints | Must simulate team-level qualification and active starts. |
| team_save_pct, team_gaa (D) | Sum saves / sum shots against; 60 × sum GA / sum goalie minutes | Never mean individual ratios without exposure weights. |

## 8. Player projections and category output columns

For each *counted* stat k, retain:
- observed historical count/rate/exposure and sources;
- posterior/shrunk rate per state; expected conditional event rate;
- expected appearances and state minutes;
- expected total, P10/P50/P90, predictive variance and calibration diagnostics;
- joint simulation draw IDs (or reproducible seed) so counts and ratios co-vary;
- flags for fallback prior, unknown availability, sparse minutes, source incompatibility, role shock and missing data.

Projected counting events must be >=0 and coherent at the simulation-draw level. P=G+A; PPP <= P and event partitions must reconcile. Predictions of plus/minus, rare bonuses and save rates require separate evaluation. Do not report decimal projections as guaranteed realized outcomes.

## 9. Draft-value derived variables

| Variable / role | Definition and methodological notes |
|---|---|
| projected_fantasy_points (D) | Sum exact platform weight × modeled event + scored bonuses/penalties. Valid only for point formats. |
| category_expected_count_k (D) | Model forecast for scored category k within relevant window and usable roster games. |
| category_z_k (D) | (projected count - selected eligible-reference mean) / reference SD, adjusted for direction. Preliminary descriptive baseline; not probability of winning. |
| category_win_prob_k (D) | H2H modeled probability of beating representative or known opponent with ties handled per league. |
| category_marginal_win_prob_k (D) | Change in win probability after adding player and reoptimizing lineup. Include team/roster correlations. |
| team_ratio_delta_k (D) | Recompute ratio from team numerator and denominator before/after feasible player replacement. |
| intrinsic_fantasy_value (D) | Scoring-rule translation before replacement and acquisition cost. |
| replacement_reference_by_position (D) | Feasible attainable player/combination for roster slot(s), not arbitrary generic league average. |
| vorp_or_replacement_surplus (D) | Incremental fantasy value vs feasible replacement, with same units and horizon. |
| roster_marginal_value (D) | Utility(optimized roster with candidate) - Utility(optimized roster without candidate). Recompute as roster changes. |
| roster_fit_by_category (D) | Category-level marginal team contribution; not a hand-set need bonus. |
| usable_start_fraction (D) | Forecast active appearances / forecast NHL appearances given lineup limits and game collisions. |
| preseason_adp, adp_distribution, adp_as_of (D/A) | Platform-specific market pick evidence; preserve source, date and format. |
| adp_gap_picks (D) | Difference between own predicted rank and market ADP, in **pick** units, not fantasy z-score units. |
| p_available_next_pick (D) | Estimated from ADP/pick-distribution/draft context; report assumptions and uncertainty. |
| pick_opportunity_cost (D) | Utility forgone by drafting candidate instead of alternatives available now/next pick. |
| draft_surplus_utility (D) | Same-unit expected roster utility net of feasible acquisition alternatives; cannot be raw z-score - ADP rank. |
| uncertainty_p10/p50/p90 (D) | Quantiles of joint modeled **roster** value; scenarios must represent GP+role+rate correlations. |
| tier, tier_confidence, explanation (D) | Similar-valued groups with uncertainty and reason codes; rankings can change within indistinguishable tiers. |

**Choose an optimization objective before combining values.** Possible objectives include projected points, expected roto standings points, H2H category wins, league-title probability (requires careful simulation), or regular-season/playoff utility. Do not conflate these. A position-adjusted score is not necessarily a calibrated championship probability.

## 10. Interactions and double-counting hazards

1. **GP × conditional TOI × state event rate:** no additional generic "durability multiplier" after GP is already reduced for missed games.
2. **Shots, xG and goals:** predictive inputs may correlate; model conditional information and out-of-sample gain; don't independently award direct fantasy points for xG unless league scores xG.
3. **Goals, assists, points and PPP:** if a league scores all of them, applying each actual scoring rule is correct; treating their simulations as independent is not.
4. **PP1 probability and PP TOI:** avoid adding both arbitrary PP1 boost and already role-adjusted PP minutes.
5. **Multi-position eligibility:** player can fill one slot per game, not two simultaneously; flexible-slot replacement can create non-obvious scarcity.
6. **Goals against, saves, goalie SV% and GAA:** forecast count/denominator processes jointly; ratios recomputed from summed counts and minutes.
7. **ADP:** affects price/availability only, not intrinsic skill. Model surplus and ADP gap are different units.
8. **Playoff games and regular season games:** separate objectives; don't add playoff games to regular-season projections.
9. **Future info:** last known team, PP unit, injury status and platform eligibility must be frozen at every historical cutoff.
10. **Availability and quality:** missing row != zero events; unknown status != healthy; raw report populations can differ. Preserve all player IDs and report exceptions.

## 11. MVP feature set, richer feature set, and gates

**MVP:** player/season ID; as-of team and fantasy position; multi-season GP, EV/PP/SH TOI; goals, primary/secondary assists if available, shots, PPP; all actual peripheral categories; age and rolling opportunity; scheduled games; explicit sparse-history priors; distinct goalie starts and counting/ratio outcomes. This can support an interpretable, leakage-aware baseline.

**Candidate enhancements:** individual xG, attempted shots and locations; on-ice scoring/teammate context; timestamped lines and PP roles; timestamped injuries; team pace/PP opportunity forecasts; NHL EDGE; goalie shot quality and tandem forecasts; weekly lineup optimizer; realistic ADP survival and other drafter models. Admit an enhancement only after testing that it improves held-out event or decision-level performance.

**Required checks before board export:** complete source/season/format metadata; no duplicate player×cutoff keys; clear injury unknowns; valid TOI partitions; nonnegative counts; P=G+A; PPP<=P; valid goalie save identities; ratio denominator safety; feasible roster slots; no future leakage; category scoring unit tests; ADP only compared in common units; baseline/out-of-sample metrics attached. Never call an unbacktested number a validated projection.

## 12. Suggested validation outputs

- forecast_cutoffs.csv: timestamps, source versions and model version per test.
- event_errors_by_position.csv: count MAE, bias, uncertainty coverage and sparse-history stratification.
- opportunity_errors.csv: GP/start and TOI MAE, calibration and role-transition errors.
- fantasy_category_calibration.csv: totals, ratio denominators and H2H category probability calibration.
- mock_draft_ablation.csv: points-only vs opportunity vs multi-category vs replacement vs draft-aware vs roster-aware, tested on matched historical drafts.
- draft_board.csv: only after league rules and model gates are satisfied; retain explanation/provenance columns.

All paths are proposals. Existing repo data and analysis scripts must remain intact.
