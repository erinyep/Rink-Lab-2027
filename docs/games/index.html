How to read the predictions

Updated team goal and win model adds fitted team strength, xG, home ice and rest. The cards below retain the original player-sum goal scenarios and shot models for comparison.

  1. Projected goals are averages. A 3.6–2.8 projection means one team has a higher scoring expectation; it is not an exact final-score call.
  2. The lean follows that difference. It is a simple model scoring comparison, not a validated chance of winning.
  3. Check shots and goalie scenarios. Volume adds context. Goalie starters and NHL lineups are unconfirmed in these saved inputs.

Calibrated opponent shots is selected by default for the all-player NHL SOG view based on the season-to-date paired evaluation. Fantasy shots keep the existing default policy. Other saved models remain available for comparison.
