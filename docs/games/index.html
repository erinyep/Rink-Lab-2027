Model evidence for today’s release

Across 84 saved November games and 3,013 matched player-games, calibrated-opponent shots had 1.010 MAE versus 1.026 for default (1.5% lower; 67/84 games lower; game-cluster 95% interval [-0.020, -0.011]). It remains selected for NHL-wide SOG. Fantasy shots keep their separate default policy.

The isolated usage-only ATOI model reduced conditional ice-time MAE from 2.204 to 2.058 minutes across 55 prospective games (52/55 games lower; paired 95% interval [-0.171, -0.122]). It is selected for future conditional ATOI projections. Assist and team-probability results do not support another policy change.

How to read the predictions

Updated team goal and win model adds fitted team strength, xG, home ice and rest. The cards below retain the original player-sum goal scenarios and shot models for comparison.

  1. Projected goals are averages. A 3.6–2.8 projection means one team has a higher scoring expectation; it is not an exact final-score call.
  2. The lean follows that difference. It is a simple model scoring comparison, not a validated chance of winning.
  3. Check shots and goalie scenarios. Volume adds context. Goalie starters and NHL lineups are unconfirmed in these saved inputs.
